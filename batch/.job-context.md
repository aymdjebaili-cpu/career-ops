Weinheim Corteco GmbH Freudenberg Aftermarket (FAM) is a newly established Business Group bringing together aftermarket activities from across the Freudenberg Group into one focused global organization. We are looking for a finance professional who combines strong analytical capabilities with accounting expertise, business acumen, and a continuous improvement mindset. In this role, you will help establish the financial reporting and accounting governance foundations that support a growing international business. Financial Analyst Group Reporting, Consolidation & Accounting Governance (f/m/d) Responsibilities Drive Financial Transparency : Ensure accurate, reliable, and timely financial reporting across the Business Group while supporting key management and corporate reporting requirements. Support Consolidation & Closing Activities : Contribute to monthly and quarterly consolidation processes, ensuring data integrity, consistency, and compliance with Group standards. Strengthen Accounting Governance : Coordinate key accounting topics across international entities and support the resolution of complex accounting and audit-related matters. Provide Meaningful Insights : Transform financial data into clear analyses and management information that support sound business decisions. Partner Across the Organization : Collaborate with Finance teams, Shared Services, auditors, and business stakeholders to ensure alignment and effective issue resolution. Drive Process Excellence : Identify opportunities to improve reporting, closing, and governance processes through standardization, digitalization, and continuous improvement. Qualifications University degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration , or a related field. Extensive years of experience in group reporting, consolidation, financial controlling, accounting governance, or corporate finance within an international environment. Strong knowledge of IFRS, consolidation principles, financial reporting, month-end closing, provisions, accruals, and balance sheet management . Proven ability to analyze complex financial topics, challenge assumptions, and deliver high-quality results with accuracy and ownership . Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills , combined with a solid understanding of business impact. Experience partnering across multiple entities, functions, and stakeholders in an international organization. Demonstrated ability to drive process improvements, digitalization initiatives, and finance transformation projects . Strong command of Excel , financial systems, and reporting tools; experience with HFM/Hyperion, Power BI, SAP BI, or similar platforms is an advantage. Fluent English ; German is an advantage.
